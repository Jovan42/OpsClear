# Feedback and credits

Found something that doesn't work, or have an idea that would make OpsClear better for you? Tell the OpsClear team from the **Feedback** page. Every submission is read, and a helpful one can earn your organisation a credit toward a future payment.

<FeatureLink card="feedback-credits" />

Everyone in the organisation can send feedback, whatever their role.

## Send a bug report or an idea

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Feedback">
    Click your name at the top right and choose **Feedback**. The page opens on **My submissions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new submission">
    Click **New submission**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Under **Type**, choose **Bug** for something that doesn't work, **Feature** for an idea, or **Other** for anything else. For **Bug** and **Feature**, the **Description** box fills in with a few headings to answer, and the **What to include** box on the right says what each heading asks for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Give it a short **Title**, then write the **Description** under the headings. For a bug, say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, including any message you saw.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Click **Submit feedback**. The button works once the title and the description are both filled in. You go back to **My submissions**, where your new submission is at the top.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/feedback-1.webp" alt="The New submission tab with Bug chosen, a title, a description with the headings Steps to reproduce, Expected behavior and Actual behavior filled in, the What to include box on the right and a Submit feedback button" caption="A bug report: the headings in the description match the What to include box." />

<Callout type="tip">
If you change the type before you've written anything, the headings change with it. Once you've typed in the description, changing the type keeps your text.
</Callout>

## Your submissions

**My submissions** lists everything you have sent, newest first. Each one shows its type, its status, the title, what you wrote and the day you sent it. You only see your own submissions here, not the ones other people in your organisation sent, and a submission can't be changed once it's sent.

The status tells you where it stands:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Pending review** | Sent, and waiting for the OpsClear team |
| **Credit granted** | The team found it helpful and gave your organisation a credit |
| **Declined** | The team looked at it and gave no credit for it |

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/feedback-2.webp" alt="My submissions with two entries: a new bug report marked Pending review, and an earlier feature idea marked Credit granted" caption="A new bug report waiting for review, above an earlier idea that earned a credit." />

When the team has looked at your submission, you get a **Feedback resolved** [notification](/guide/notifications). It says whether your submission earned a credit, and clicking it opens **Feedback**.

## How credits work

Credits are the OpsClear team's way of saying thank you. The team decides case by case, by hand. A credit is never given automatically, and not every submission gets one, even a good one.

- A credit goes to your organisation, not to you personally.
- It is an amount in euros, taken off a future payment for your organisation's [subscription](/guide/subscription).
- The OpsClear team can also give a credit for another reason, not only for feedback.

## Your credit balance

<RoleBadge role={['Owner', 'Admin']} />

Click your name at the top right and choose **Organisation**. On **Organisation Settings**, the **Credit balance** section shows how much credit your organisation has, in euros. It updates by itself when a credit is added or used, and Members don't see it.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/feedback-3.webp" alt="The Credit balance section on Organisation Settings, showing a balance of 20" caption="The Credit balance section shows what your organisation can still use." />

When the Owner changes the plan and a charge is due straight away, the window that shows the cost lists **Credit applied** if part of it is paid with credit.
