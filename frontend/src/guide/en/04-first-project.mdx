# Create a project and add members

A project holds the jobs for one piece of work, such as a client, a site or a campaign. It has its own jobs, its own people and its own dashboard. Create one for each separate piece of work you want to keep an eye on.

## Create a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the projects page">
    After you sign in you land on **Projects**. If you have none yet, click **Create your first project**. Otherwise click **+ New Project**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the project">
    Type a **Name**, for example "Kitchen refit". You can also add a **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add ready-made block reasons (optional)">
    Under **Block reasons** you can type reasons your team often needs, such as "Waiting on client". People pick from this list when they block a job. See [When a job is blocked](/guide/job-blocked).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create project**. The project opens and you're its Owner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Project roles

Everyone in a project has one of three roles in that project. It's separate from their role in the organisation, so the same person can be an Owner of one project and a Member of another.

| Role | What they do in the project |
|------|-----------------------------|
| Owner | Whoever created the project. Manages the project and its people, and can complete or delete it |
| Admin | Manages jobs and people, and approves requests |
| Member | Works on the jobs assigned to them, and sees only those jobs |

For the full picture see [Roles and permissions](/guide/roles-permissions).

## Add members

Only people who are already in your organisation can be added, so invite them first (see [Invite your team](/guide/invite-team)).

<RoleBadge role={['Owner', 'Admin']} />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project settings">
    In the project's top bar, click **Settings**, then find the **Members** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the person">
    Start typing their email address in **Search by email…** and click them in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role and add them">
    Pick **Member**, **Admin** or **Owner** in the role box next to the search, then click **Add**. They appear in the members table and can open the project straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>
