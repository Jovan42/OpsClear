# Subscription and add-ons

Your organisation pays for OpsClear with one subscription. It has a base plan for the size of your team, and add-ons for the extra features you want. You can change your choice later.

<RoleBadge role="Owner" />

Only the Owner can set up or change the subscription and see the billing. Everyone else just uses what the plan includes.

## Set up your subscription

A new organisation has no subscription, and projects stay locked until the Owner sets one up. Until then, everyone except the Owner sees **Subscription required**, and the Owner sees **Set up your subscription**. Later you find the same section in **Organisation Settings**, under **Subscription**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the base plan">
    Under **Base plan**, drag **Team members** and **Active projects** to the size you need. **Base price** shows what that costs each month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose add-ons">
    Under **Add-ons**, click each feature you want. A chosen card has a green border, and its price is added to the total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick monthly or yearly">
    Switch on **Annual billing** to pay for the year and save. The total shows the monthly amount either way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to payment">
    Check the **Monthly total**, then click **Continue to payment**. A payment form opens on the page. Fill it in and pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait a moment">
    **Processing your payment** appears until the payment is confirmed, which can take a moment. The page updates by itself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note">
If you see "This plan isn't available for checkout yet", try again a little later.
</Callout>

## The add-ons

Every add-on adds one feature. Without it, that feature is locked.

| Add-on | What it gives you |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Dashboard** | The [dashboard](/guide/dashboard) and the project [stats](/guide/stats-overview) |
| **Approvals** | [Approval requests](/guide/job-approval) and the rule that jobs need approval to complete |
| **Notes** | [Notes](/guide/notes-links-history) on jobs |
| **Job status history** | The log of every [status change](/guide/notes-links-history) |
| **Milestones** | [Milestones](/guide/milestones) that group jobs into phases |
| **Job relationships** | [Links between jobs](/guide/job-relationships) |
| **API keys** | [Keys](/guide/api-keys) to connect other programs |
| **Job templates** | [Templates](/guide/templates-and-types) for jobs you create often |
| **Recurring scheduling** | [Recurring jobs](/guide/recurring-jobs) created on a schedule |
| **Job links** | [Web links](/guide/notes-links-history) on jobs and projects |
| **Job types** | [Job types](/guide/templates-and-types) to sort jobs |

The add-on names stay in English in both languages. **See all features →** next to **Subscription** opens a page that shows each feature at work.

## Change your plan

Once you're paying, change the sliders, the add-ons or the billing period and click **Save subscription**. A window shows what happens before anything changes.

- **Adding or upgrading** is charged right away, for the rest of the current billing period. The window shows **Charged now**, and **Credit applied** if you have a credit. Click **Confirm change**.
- **Removing or downsizing** costs nothing now. You keep your current plan until the period ends, then the new plan starts. A **Plan change scheduled** box shows what's coming. Click **Cancel pending downgrade** to keep the current plan.

You can't add something and remove something in the same change. OpsClear says so, and you save the two changes one after the other.

## Billing

The **Billing** box shows whether the subscription is **Active** or **Past due**.

- **Update payment method** opens the payment form to change your card.
- **Cancel subscription** stops it from renewing. It stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and then the add-on features are locked. Until then, **Resume subscription** takes the cancellation back at no cost.

<Callout type="warning">
If a payment doesn't go through, the box says **Past due** and the Owner and Admins get a **Subscription past due** notification. Everyone can still see their data, but features that need an add-on can't be changed until the payment is sorted out. Click **Update payment method** to fix it.
</Callout>

## Your payments

Click **Billing history** to open the list. Each payment shows its date, its status and the amount.

## When a feature is locked

A feature your organisation hasn't paid for shows a small lock in the project's top bar, or a button to upgrade where it would appear on a job. Click the lock and you see "is not included in your plan" and the button **Upgrade in org settings**. The Owner can use it to add the feature. Anyone else should ask the Owner.
