# Your preferences

Preferences are personal. They change how OpsClear looks and behaves for you, and nobody else sees the difference. Use them to pick your language, to open the page you use most, or to hide what you don't need.

## Open your settings

Click your name at the top right and choose **Account settings**. The **Settings** page lists its sections on the left: **Appearance**, **Jobs**, **Dashboard**, **Navigation**, **Milestones**, **Stats**, **Notifications**, **Security** and **API keys**. Click one to jump to it.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/preferences-1.webp" alt="The Settings page with the list of sections on the left and the Appearance section open: Theme, Language, Date format and Row density, each with its own control" caption="Settings, with the list of sections on the left." />

A change is saved as soon as you make it. It's stored on your account, so it applies on every device you sign in on. If a change can't be saved, you see "Couldn't save your preferences".

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**, which follows your device.
- **Language**: **English** or **Srpski**. The whole app changes.
- **Date format**: **Auto** follows the language. Or choose **DD.MM.YYYY**, **DD/MM/YYYY** or **MM/DD/YYYY**.
- **Row density**: **Comfortable** or **Compact** spacing in the job table on a computer.

## Jobs

These decide what you see when you open a project's job list. See [Finding your jobs](/guide/finding-jobs).

- **Default view mode**: **Grouped** or **Flat**.
- **Default status tab**: the tab selected first, from **All** to **Completed**.
- **Completed jobs in All tab**: **Show** or **Hide** them.
- **Default sort order**: **Deadline ↑**, **Deadline ↓**, **Priority** or **Newest first**.
- **Default assignee filter**: **Everyone** or **Mine**.
- **Deadline format**: **Absolute** or **Relative**.
- **Due-soon highlight**: how many days ahead a deadline turns amber, or **Off**. Overdue deadlines stay red.

## Dashboard

- **Blocked jobs section**, **Overdue jobs section** and **Pending approvals section**: **Show** or **Hide** each list on the [dashboard](/guide/dashboard).
- **Show approvals on Overview**: **On** or **Off**. Only Owners and Admins of the organisation see this one. The approvals badge in the menu stays either way. See [Stats and the Overview](/guide/stats-overview).

## Navigation

- **Landing project**: where you land after signing in: the **Project list**, the **Last opened** project, or a **Specific** one. For a specific project, choose it in **Project**. A link you opened before signing in still takes priority.
- **Default project page**: **Dashboard**, **Jobs** or **Approvals**, the page that opens when you go to a project.

## Milestones and stats

- **Milestone accordion**: milestone groups start **Expanded** or **Collapsed**.
- **Progress format**: milestone progress as a **Fraction** such as 4/6, or a **Percentage**.
- **Default range** under **Stats**: **4 weeks**, **12 weeks** or **26 weeks**. Changing the range on the Stats page itself lasts only until you leave it.

## Notifications

**Notification sound** plays a short sound when a new notification arrives, and each type of notification has a switch. See [Notifications](/guide/notifications).

## Security: change your password

You can change your password while you're signed in, without an email. Anyone can do it, whatever their role.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security">
    Click your name at the top right and choose **Account settings**, then click **Security** in the list of sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change password">
    Next to **Password**, click **Change password**. The **Update password** page opens, the same OpsClear sign-in page you use to log in. If you signed in more than a few minutes ago, it first asks you to sign in again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the new password">
    Type the new password in **New password** and again in **Confirm password**. It needs at least 8 characters. **Sign out from other devices** is ticked, so other computers and phones have to sign in again with the new password. Untick it to stay signed in there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit**. You're back on **Settings** and see "Your password has been changed." Use the new password the next time you sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changed your mind? Click **Cancel** on the **Update password** page. You're back on **Settings** with "Password change cancelled. Your password stays the same."

Forgot your password and can't sign in? See "I forgot my password" in [FAQ and troubleshooting](/guide/faq).
