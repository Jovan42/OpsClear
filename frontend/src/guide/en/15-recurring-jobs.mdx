# Recurring jobs

Some jobs come back again and again: a monthly boiler service, a weekly safety check. A schedule creates them for you on a timetable, so nobody has to remember. Each new job is built from a [job template](/guide/templates-and-types).

<FeatureLink card="recurring" />

<Callout type="note">
Recurring jobs are an add-on, and they need the job templates add-on too. Without them, the **Schedules** page says it isn't included in your plan. See [Subscription and add-ons](/guide/subscription).
</Callout>

## See your schedules

Open your project and click **Schedules** in the top bar. Each schedule shows its name and a status label, the template it uses, a short description of when it runs, the next run and the people it hands jobs to.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/recurring-jobs-1.webp" alt="The Schedules page with two schedules, one Paused and one Active, each showing its template, when it runs, the next run and a circle for each person in its rotation, with a banner under the second one saying 1 missed run" caption="Each schedule shows its status, its template and when it runs next." />

The status label tells you what a schedule is doing:

- **Active**: it creates jobs on time.
- **Paused**: it is switched off for now, and no jobs are created.
- **No assignees**: it paused itself because everyone in its rotation left the project.
- **Expired**: its end date has passed.

## Create a schedule

<RoleBadge role={['Owner', 'Admin']} />

You need at least one template first. Open **Templates** and create one, or click **Schedule** next to an existing template to start with it already chosen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new schedule">
    On the **Schedules** page, click **+ New Schedule**. If there are none yet, click **Create first schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and choose the template">
    Type a **Name** and pick the **Template** the jobs are made from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it runs">
    Under **Schedule**, choose **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly** and set the day and the time. **Advanced** is for people who know how to write a cron timetable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    The line under the time says in words when the schedule runs, and **Next occurrences** lists the coming dates. Check that they are what you expect, and pick the **Timezone** the times refer to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people (optional)">
    Under **Assignee rotation**, add the people who should get the jobs. They take turns in the order you list them: the first job goes to the first person, the next to the second, and so on, starting again at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pause or end it (optional)">
    Use **Pause until** to start the schedule on a later date, and **Expires at** to stop it on a date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/recurring-jobs-2.webp" alt="The New Schedule window with the name, the template menu, the Daily, Weekly, Monthly and Advanced choices, a weekly schedule on Monday, a line in words saying when it runs, and the list of next occurrences" caption="The preview shows in words when the schedule runs and lists the next dates." />

Jobs a schedule creates look like any other job, with the note **Created by schedule** on the job's page. The person it is assigned to gets a notification.

## Pause, resume, edit or delete

On a schedule's row, click **Pause** and choose for how long: **1 day**, **1 week**, **1 month**, a **Custom date** or **Indefinitely**. Click **Resume** to start it again. **Edit** changes any of its settings, and **Delete** removes it after you confirm.

<Callout type="warning">
Deleting a schedule stops new jobs from being created. The jobs it already created are kept.
</Callout>

## Missed runs

If OpsClear couldn't create a job at the planned time, for example because it was down for maintenance, it doesn't flood your project with late jobs. It keeps a note instead, and the schedule shows a banner such as **1 missed run**. Click the banner to open the list.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/recurring-jobs-3.webp" alt="The missed run banner opened under a schedule, listing one missed run with the buttons Create job and Dismiss" caption="Open the banner to decide what to do with each missed run." />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at the missed runs">
    Click the banner. Each missed run is listed with the date it should have happened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide for each one">
    Click **Create job** to make the job now, or **Dismiss** if you don't need it. With several missed runs, **Dismiss all** clears them in one go.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Owners and Admins are told about a missed run in their [notifications](/guide/notifications).
