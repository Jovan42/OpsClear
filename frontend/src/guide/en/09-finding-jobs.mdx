# Finding your jobs

The **Jobs** list is where you spend most of your day. As a project grows it fills up, so OpsClear gives you a few quick ways to narrow it down to the jobs you care about right now.

Owners and Admins see every job in the project. A Member only sees the jobs assigned to them.

## Status tabs

Under the filters, one tab per status shows how many jobs are in it: **All**, **New**, **In Progress**, **Blocked** and **Completed**. Click a tab to see only those jobs. A tab with no jobs is greyed out.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/finding-jobs-2.webp" alt="The Jobs list with the In Progress tab selected, showing only jobs that are In Progress" caption="Click a tab to see only the jobs with that status." />

Each row shows the job's title, client, who it's assigned to, deadline, priority and status. Click a row to open the job. A red bar on the left of a row marks a blocked job.

## Search and filter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type in the search box at the top. It looks in the job's title, the client and the name of the person it's assigned to, and the list updates as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by priority">
    Use the **All priorities** menu to show only **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Critical** jobs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by type or milestone">
    If your project uses job types, the **All types** menu shows one type at a time. If it has milestones, the **All milestones** menu shows the jobs of one milestone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show only your jobs">
    Click **Mine** at the right of the tabs to see only the jobs assigned to you. The numbers on the tabs change to match. Click **Everyone** to see all jobs again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/finding-jobs-3.webp" alt="The Jobs list with Mine selected, showing only the jobs assigned to the signed-in person and smaller counts on the tabs" caption="With Mine selected, only your own jobs are listed." />

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/finding-jobs-4.webp" alt="The Jobs list after typing a word in the search box, showing only the matching jobs" caption="The search narrows the list as you type." />

The search, the filters and the tabs all work together. If nothing matches, the list says **No jobs match this filter.**

<Callout type="note">
Job types and milestones are add-ons, so those two menus only appear when your organisation has them. See [Subscription and add-ons](/guide/subscription).
</Callout>

## Grouped or flat

When the project has [milestones](/guide/milestones), the jobs are grouped under them, with a progress bar for each milestone. Click the **Grouped** button to switch to one plain list (the button then reads **Flat**), and click it again to go back. Click a milestone's heading to fold it up or open it.

## Sort the list

Click a column heading, such as **Deadline** or **Priority**, to sort by it. An arrow shows the direction. Click the same heading again to reverse it. Jobs with no deadline always come last when you sort by deadline.

## Late and nearly late jobs

The deadline tells you how urgent a job is without opening it:

- A deadline in red means the job is overdue.
- A deadline in amber with a **Due soon** label means the deadline is close.

<Callout type="tip">
You choose how close "close" is, or turn the label off, in your own settings. Click your name at the top right, choose **Account settings** and look in the **Jobs** section. See [Your preferences](/guide/preferences). The same section sets which tab the list opens on, and whether it starts on **Everyone** or **Mine**.
</Callout>
