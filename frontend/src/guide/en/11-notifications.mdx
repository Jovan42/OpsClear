# Notifications

Notifications tell you when something happens that you need to know about: a job is assigned to you, a job is blocked, an approval is waiting. You don't have to keep checking. They arrive as they happen, without reloading the page.

<FeatureLink card="notifications" />

## The bell

The bell at the top right of every page shows how many notifications you haven't read yet. Click it to open the latest ones.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bell">
    Click the bell. The newest notifications are listed, and unread ones have a blue dot. Urgent ones, such as a blocked job, have a red bar and red text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open what it's about">
    Click a notification to go straight to the job. It is marked as read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear the rest">
    Click **Mark all as read** to clear the dots, or **View all** to open the full page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Notifications page

The **Notifications** page keeps the full list, newest first.

- **All** and **Unread** switch between everything and what you haven't read yet.
- **All types** lets you pick one kind of notification, such as **Job blocked**.
- **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread** at the right of a row changes just that one. Use it to keep something as a reminder.
- **Mark all as read** at the top clears everything.

## What you are told about

What reaches you depends on your role and on the job. Owners and Admins of a project hear about more than Members do, because they look after the whole project.

| Notification | When you get it |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **Job assigned** | A job is assigned to you |
| **Status changed** | A job you're responsible for changes status. Owners and Admins hear about every job in the project |
| **Job blocked** | A job you're responsible for is blocked. Owners and Admins hear about every blocked job |
| **Blocker cleared** | A job that was holding up your job is completed |
| **Deadline or priority changed** | Someone changes the deadline or priority of your job |
| **Job deleted** | Your job is deleted |
| **Note added** | Someone adds a note to your job, or to a job where you wrote a note |
| **Deadline tomorrow** | Your job is due tomorrow |
| **Job overdue** | The deadline of your job has passed and it isn't completed. Owners and Admins hear about every late job |
| **Approval requested** | Someone asks for approval. Owners and Admins only |
| **Approval decided** | An approval you asked for is approved or rejected |
| **Added to project** | You're added to a project |
| **Schedule run missed** | A [recurring job](/guide/recurring-jobs) didn't run. Owners and Admins only |
| **Invite accepted** | Someone you invited joins the organisation |
| **Feedback resolved** | Your feedback to OpsClear was reviewed, and may have earned a credit |
| **Subscription past due** | The organisation's payment didn't go through. Owners and Admins only |

<Callout type="note">
You only get a notification for something you're allowed to see, and for a feature your organisation has. For example, you won't hear about notes if the Notes add-on isn't active. See [Subscription and add-ons](/guide/subscription).
</Callout>

## Mute a type or turn off the sound

If a type is too chatty, mute it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your settings">
    Click your name at the top right and choose **Account settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the notification types">
    Open the **Notifications** section. Under **Notification types**, each kind of notification has a switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch a type off">
    Turn off the types you don't want. The change is saved straight away and applies on all your devices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Under **Notification sound** you can choose **On** or **Off** for the short sound that plays when a new notification arrives in the tab you're using.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/notifications-4.webp" alt="The Notifications part of the settings page: the Notification sound switch set to On, and the list of Notification types, each with a switch" caption="Each notification type has its own switch. The sound has its own setting above." />

<Callout type="warning">
A type you switch off isn't just hidden. You don't receive those notifications at all, so you can't read them later. **Subscription past due** can't be switched off, because it affects the whole organisation.
</Callout>
