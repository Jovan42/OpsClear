# Notes, links and history

Every job page keeps its own record. Use **Notes** for what was said and agreed, **Links** for the documents and files that belong to the job, and **Status history** to see who moved the job and when. Together they answer "what happened here?" without a phone call.

<FeatureLink card="notes" />

<Callout type="note">
Notes, links and the status history are separate add-ons. If your organisation doesn't have one, its section is hidden or marked as not included. See [Subscription and add-ons](/guide/subscription).
</Callout>

## Notes

A note is a message on the job: a decision, an agreement, a change of plan. Everyone who works on the job can read the notes and add their own. Each note shows who wrote it and when.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job">
    Open the job from the **Jobs** list. If the **Notes** section is folded up, click its heading to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the note">
    Type in the box under the notes. You can format the text with the buttons above the box, and use **Preview** to see how it will look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Add Note**. A window reminds you that notes can't be edited or deleted, once each time you open OpsClear in the browser. Click **Add Note** again to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
A note can never be changed or deleted, not even by an Owner. That is what makes it a reliable record. If you made a mistake, add a new note that corrects it.
</Callout>

A note can be up to 10,000 characters long. People who have written a note on a job, and the person it's assigned to, get a notification when someone adds another one. See [Notifications](/guide/notifications).

## Links

Links put the things a job depends on in one place: a drawing, a supplier order, a shared folder, a web page. They sit in the **Links** section of the job.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Links section">
    On the job's page, open **Links**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a link">
    Click **+ Add link**. Paste the address, starting with https://, and type a short name in **Label (optional)**. For well-known services the name is filled in for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Save**. The link appears in the list with the name of the person who added it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click a link's name to open it in a new tab. The buttons at its right copy the address, edit the link and remove it. Anyone on the job can add a link, but only Owners and Admins can edit or remove one.

## Status history

The **Status history** section lists every time the job's status changed, newest last. For each change you see the old and new status, who made it and when. It also shows how long the job stayed in each status, and the reason when a job was blocked.

Use it to answer questions such as how long a job waited, or who unblocked it. You can't change it: it fills in by itself as the job moves along.
