# A job from start to finish

A job's status tells everyone where the work stands. Most jobs take the same short path: **New**, then **In Progress**, then **Completed**. This page follows one job along it.

<FeatureLink card="job-tracking" />

<RoleBadge role={['Owner', 'Admin']} />

The person the job is assigned to can change its status too. Other Members can't.

## Move the job through its statuses

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job">
    Open the **Jobs** list and click the job's title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Click **Start** when work begins. The status changes from **New** to **In Progress**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note">
    Open the **Notes** section, type what happened, for example "Parts delivered, starting on site tomorrow morning.", and click **Add Note**. A window reminds you that notes can't be edited or deleted. Click **Add Note** again to save it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete it">
    When the work is finished, click **Complete**, then **Mark Complete** to confirm. The status becomes **Completed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note">
Notes and the status history are add-ons. If your organisation doesn't have one, that section is hidden or marked as not included. See [Subscription and add-ons](/guide/subscription).
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
In a project that asks for approval before jobs are completed, a Member sees **Request completion** instead of **Complete**. See [When a job needs approval](/guide/job-approval).
</Callout>

## Change your mind

Finished by mistake? An Owner or Admin can click **Reopen** on a completed job to put it back to **In Progress**. The person it's assigned to can't undo a completion themselves.

## See it on the dashboard

The project's **Dashboard** shows the whole picture. A ring chart and six counts show how many jobs are **New**, **In Progress**, **Blocked** and **Completed**, how many are **Overdue**, and how many approvals are waiting. The counts update as statuses change, and the **Completed** count goes up when you finish a job.
