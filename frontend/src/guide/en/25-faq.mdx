# FAQ and troubleshooting

Something not working as you expect? Look for your question below.

## Signing in

### I forgot my password

On the sign-in page, click **Forgot Password?**, type your email address and click **Submit**. OpsClear emails you a link called **Reset password**. Open it within 5 minutes, choose a new password of at least 8 characters, and click **Submit**. If you don't want to be signed out on your other devices, untick **Sign out from other devices** first. The email is called "Reset your OpsClear password". If you didn't ask for it, ignore the email and your password stays the same.

### The verification link has expired

The link in the **Verify your email for OpsClear** email works for 5 minutes. Sign in, and on the **Verify your email** page click **Resend email**. More in [Sign up and verify your email](/guide/sign-up).

### I typed the wrong password several times and can't sign in

After several wrong passwords in a row, signing in is paused for a short time. Wait a little, then try again. If you can't remember the password, use **Forgot Password?**.

### I was signed out

OpsClear signs you out after a long time away. Sign in again and carry on.

## Invites and joining

### My invite doesn't work

- The invite only works for the email address it was sent to. If you signed up with another address, you see "This invite was sent to a different email address". Ask the Owner or an Admin to invite the address you use.
- An invite lasts 7 days, and works once. If it has expired or been used, ask for a new one.

### I sent an invite and nothing arrived

Ask your colleague to check spam. Then click **Resend email** next to the invite. You can resend an invite once every 5 minutes, and OpsClear shows how long to wait. Or click **Copy link** and send it yourself. See [Invite your team](/guide/invite-team).

## Can't see what you expect

### I can't see any projects

You only see projects you're a member of. Ask an Owner or Admin of the project to add you in its settings. Being a member of the organisation isn't enough. See [Create a project and add members](/guide/first-project).

### I can only see some of the jobs

A Member only sees the jobs assigned to them. Ask an Owner or Admin to assign you the job you need. See [Roles and permissions](/guide/roles-permissions).

### I see "Subscription required"

A new organisation has no subscription, and projects stay locked until the Owner sets one up. Ask the Owner to open **Organisation** and do it. See [Subscription and add-ons](/guide/subscription).

### A feature has a lock, or says "is not included in your plan"

Your organisation doesn't have the add-on for that feature. The Owner can add it under **Subscription** in the organisation settings. See [Subscription and add-ons](/guide/subscription).

### I can't find the Overview

The **Overview** is only for Owners and Admins of the organisation. See [Stats and the Overview](/guide/stats-overview).

### I can't complete a job

Only the person the job is assigned to, and Owners and Admins, can change its status. In a project that asks for approval, a Member sees **Request completion** instead of **Complete**. See [When a job needs approval](/guide/job-approval).

### I can't change a job's details, or its links

Editing a job, assigning it, and editing or removing links and relationships is for Owners and Admins. Ask one of them, or see [Roles and permissions](/guide/roles-permissions).

### A project says it no longer accepts changes

The project was completed. Its Owner can click **Reactivate** in the project's settings. See [Project settings](/guide/project-settings).

## Red messages in the corner

When something can't be done, a red message appears in a corner of the page. Its title says what kind of problem it is, such as **Forbidden**, **Conflict**, **Not Found** or **Too many requests**, and a line below says more. That line may be in English even when OpsClear is in Serbian.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/faq-1.webp" alt="A red message in the corner of the Projects page with the title Too many requests and a line saying to try again in a few seconds" caption="A red message says what went wrong and what to do." />

- **Forbidden**: your role isn't allowed to do this. Ask an Owner or Admin.
- **Conflict**: it clashes with something that already exists, for example an organisation code that is taken, or completing a project that still has open jobs.
- **Too many requests**: wait for the time the message gives, then try again.
- **Network error**: check your internet connection and try again.
- **Internal Server Error**: the problem is on our side. Wait a minute and try again.
