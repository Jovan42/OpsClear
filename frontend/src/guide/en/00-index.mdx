# Getting started with OpsClear

OpsClear answers one question for your team: **what's the truth about our work today?** Every piece of work is a *job*, and every job has one status, so you can see where things stand without calling anyone.

This page shows the whole journey from sign-up to your first tracked job. Each step has its own page with the details.

## The four job statuses

Every job is in exactly one of these four statuses. A job moves forward as work happens, and it can get blocked on the way.

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| New | The job exists but nobody has started it |
| In Progress | Someone is working on it |
| Blocked | It can't move until something is sorted out |
| Completed | It's done |

## Your first steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create your account and confirm your email address. See [Sign up and verify your email](/guide/sign-up).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your organisation">
    Give your company's workspace a name. You become its **Owner**. See [Create your organisation](/guide/create-organisation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Send an invite to each colleague's email address. See [Invite your team](/guide/invite-team).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    A project holds the jobs for one piece of work, such as a client or a site. Add the people who work on it. See [Create a project and add members](/guide/first-project).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and assign a job">
    Give the job a title, a deadline and a responsible person. See [Create and assign a job](/guide/create-job).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the job to the end">
    Start it, add a note and mark it complete. If it gets stuck, block it and say why. If it needs a decision, ask for approval. See [A job from start to finish](/guide/job-done), [When a job is blocked](/guide/job-blocked) and [When a job needs approval](/guide/job-approval).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<RoleBadge role={['Owner', 'Admin']} />

Only Owners and Admins can invite people to the organisation, so whoever signs up first usually does the inviting. Everyone you invite accepts the invite and then works on the jobs assigned to them.

<Callout type="note">
Some features need an add-on, for example the dashboard, notes and approvals. Each page tells you when something needs one. See [Subscription and add-ons](/guide/subscription).
</Callout>
