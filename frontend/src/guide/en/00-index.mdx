# Getting started with OpsClear

OpsClear answers one question for your team: **what's the truth about our work today?** Every piece of work is a *job*, and every job has one of four statuses.

<Callout type="note">
This page is a placeholder. The full Getting started guide is on its way.
</Callout>

## The four job statuses

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| New | The job exists but nobody has started it |
| In Progress | Someone is working on it |
| Blocked | It can't move until something is sorted out |
| Completed | It's done |

## Your first steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create your account and confirm your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your organisation">
    Give it a name. You become its **Owner**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Send invites from **Organisation > Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project and your first job">
    Add a job with a responsible person and a deadline.
  </Step>
</Steps>
