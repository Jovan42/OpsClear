# Sign up and verify your email

Signing up takes a minute. You create an account with your email address and a password, then confirm the address by clicking a link we email you. Until you confirm it, you can't use OpsClear.

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up form">
    On the OpsClear home page, click **Get started**. The **Register** form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Type your **First name**, **Last name** and **Email**, then choose a **Password** and type it again in **Confirm password**. Use an email address you can open right now, because the confirmation link goes there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register">
    Click **Register**. You land on the **Verify your email** page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirm your email address

The **Verify your email** page tells you where the link went. Keep it open while you check your inbox.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/sign-up-3.webp" alt="The Verify your email page: a message that a verification link was sent to your address, and a Resend email link" caption="The page says which address the link was sent to." />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the email">
    Look for an email called **Verify your email for OpsClear**. It usually arrives within a minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the button">
    Click **Verify email address** in the email. If the button doesn't work, copy the link below it into your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Carry on">
    Your address is confirmed and you're signed in. Next you create your organisation: see [Create your organisation](/guide/create-organisation).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/sign-up-4.webp" alt="The confirmation email: a welcome message, a green Verify email address button and a note that the link expires in 5 minutes" caption="The confirmation email. Click the button to finish signing up." />

<Callout type="warning">
The link expires after 5 minutes. If it has expired, use **Resend email** (below) to get a new one.
</Callout>

## If the email doesn't arrive

- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Click **Resend email** on the **Verify your email** page, next to "Didn't get the email?". A fresh link is sent.
- Typed the wrong address? Click **« Back to Login**, then register again with the right one.
- Left the page already? Log in with your email and password. The **Verify your email** page comes back, with **Resend email** on it.

<Callout type="tip">
Already have an account? Click **Log in** at the top of the home page instead of **Get started**.
</Callout>
