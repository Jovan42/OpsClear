# Create your organisation

Your organisation is your company's workspace in OpsClear. Your team, your projects, your jobs and your subscription all live inside it. Each person belongs to one organisation.

You create it right after you confirm your email address. Whoever creates it becomes its **Owner**.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name your organisation">
    Type your company or team name in **Organisation name**, for example "Acme Plumbing". You can change it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a short code">
    In **Slug**, type 2 to 3 letters, for example "ACM". It's a short code for your organisation that appears in web addresses. Letters only, and it must be different from every other organisation's code. If you see "An organisation with this slug already exists", pick another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create organisation**. Your organisation now exists and you're its Owner. Next, OpsClear asks you to set up a subscription (see below). Once that's done you land on **Organisation Settings**, where you can see your organisation's name and code and invite your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up your subscription

Before your team can use projects and jobs, the organisation needs a subscription. On the **Set up your subscription** page, choose the plan and the add-ons your team needs, then confirm with the button at the bottom of the section (**Continue to payment**). The details are on the [Subscription and add-ons](/guide/subscription) page.

<Callout type="note">
Only the Owner can set up the subscription. Anyone else who opens the app sees **Subscription required** until the Owner has done it.
</Callout>

## What it means to be the Owner

<RoleBadge role="Owner" />

As the Owner you can invite and manage people, change the organisation's settings and manage its subscription. People you invite later join as Members, and you can make some of them Admins. See [Roles and permissions](/guide/roles-permissions) for who can do what.
