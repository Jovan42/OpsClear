# Organisation settings

The organisation is your company's workspace, and its settings page is where you look after the whole of it: who is in, how it is named, how IDs look, and what you pay for.

## Open the settings

Click your name at the top right and choose **Organisation**. The **Organisation Settings** page has these sections: **Members**, **Details**, **ID prefixes**, then, depending on your role and add-ons, **Org Templates**, **Subscription**, **Billing history** and **Danger zone**.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/organisation-settings-1.webp" alt="Organisation Settings: the Members section with Manage members and Invites buttons, the Details section with the name Acme Plumbing and a three-letter code, and the start of ID prefixes" caption="The top of Organisation Settings." />

Everyone in the organisation can open the page, but Members only see the members list and the details. The details and the ID prefixes are read-only unless you are the Owner.

## Name and code

<RoleBadge role="Owner" />

In **Details**, change the **Name** or the **Slug** and click **Save changes**. The slug is the organisation's short code: 2 to 3 letters, different from every other organisation's. If the code is taken, you see "An organisation with this slug already exists".

## ID prefixes

<RoleBadge role="Owner" />

Every job, project, milestone and template has an ID such as JOB-042. **ID prefixes** lets you choose the letters in front of the number, for example TSK instead of JOB.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type the new letters">
    In **Projects**, **Jobs**, **Milestones** or **Templates**, type 2 or 3 letters. Under each box, a line such as "Next job: JOB-013" shows what the next ID will look like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep them different">
    Each type needs its own letters. If two boxes have the same letters you see "Each prefix must be different".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**, then **Change prefixes** to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only new records get the new letters. IDs that already exist keep theirs, and links to them keep working.

## Members

Click **Manage members** to open the **Members** page.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/organisation-settings-3.webp" alt="The Members page with an Invite people button above a table of four people: the Owner with a badge and three others with a role box and a Remove link" caption="The Members page lists everyone in the organisation." />

<RoleBadge role={['Owner', 'Admin']} />

- To add someone, send them an invite: click **Invite people** above the table, or **Invites** on the settings page. They join when they open the link, whether or not they already have an OpsClear account. See [Invite your team](/guide/invite-team).
- To remove someone, click **Remove** and confirm. They lose access to the organisation and its projects.
- The Owner can't be removed.

<RoleBadge role="Owner" />

Only the Owner can change a person's role, with the role box in their row. People who accept an invite join as **Members**.

<Screenshot src="/guide/en/organisation-settings-4.webp" alt="The Pending invites page with a box for an email address and a Send invite button above two waiting invites, each with Copy link, Resend email and Revoke" caption="Invites that haven't been accepted yet." />

## Templates for the whole organisation

With the Job templates add-on, **Org Templates** lists templates every project can use. Owners and Admins add, edit and delete them here. See [Job templates and job types](/guide/templates-and-types).

## Subscription and billing

<RoleBadge role="Owner" />

The Owner also sees **Subscription** and **Billing history** on this page. See [Subscription and add-ons](/guide/subscription).

## Delete the organisation

<RoleBadge role="Owner" />

At the bottom, the **Danger zone** has **Delete organisation**. Click it and confirm in the window that opens.

<Callout type="warning">
Deleting the organisation can't be undone.
</Callout>
